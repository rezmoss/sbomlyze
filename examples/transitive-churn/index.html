Transitive churn in a point release

ripgrep 14.0.3 → 14.1.1 swapped winapi for the windows-sys crate family: 11 crates added, 9 removed, new dependencies up to five levels deep.

rustsource Dependency graph diffTransitive depthmax_depth policyCargo ordering

What happened

sbomlyze reads the dependency graph from Cargo.lock (through Syft) and reports which dependency edges changed and how deep each new transitive dependency sits. Between ripgrep 14.0.3 and 14.1.1, winapi and its two target crates left, and windows-sys came in with windows-targets and eight per-target crates. packed_simd and num-traits dropped out; cc gained shlex.

At every release the changed edges are checked against both Cargo.lock files: 12 added and 16 removed must match exactly. max_depth turns depth into a gate; here the eight new dependencies at depth 5 fail it.

What this does not prove. Cargo.lock lists every target's dependencies; the windows-* crates are only built for Windows.
