A license change in a minor release

Akka 2.6.20 → 2.7.0 changed its license from Apache-2.0 to the Business Source License 1.1, and deny_licenses catches it on the upgrade.

javamaven License changedeny_licenses policyCross-format license matching

What happened

In September 2022 Lightbend moved Akka to the Business Source License 1.1. The published POMs on Maven Central say Apache-2.0 for 2.6.20 and BUSL-1.1 for 2.7.0, and Apache Pekko was forked from Akka 2.6.x because of it. For a project that only bumps a minor version, the license change arrives as an ordinary upgrade.

Syft reads the license from the jar’s manifest (Bundle-License, an OSGi field) as <url>;description=BUSL-1.1, and SPDX folds that into a LicenseRef- id. sbomlyze maps all of these to BUSL-1.1, so deny_licenses: ["BUSL-1.1"] fails the upgrade in any of the three formats. The rule checks licenses that newly appear on changed packages, not only on new ones.

What this does not prove. A minimal reproduction: the two published akka-actor jars scanned on their own. A real service would show the same change among its other dependencies.
